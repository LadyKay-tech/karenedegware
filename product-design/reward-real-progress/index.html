Principle

Reward real progress, not empty points.

Streaks and points are easy to add and easy to measure, so they turn up everywhere. They do get people opening the app. That’s rarely what anyone came for.

Someone using a savings app wants to save. A streak for opening it rewards the visit, not the saving, and one busy day wipes it out. Lose a long streak once and plenty of people quietly stop.

I’d rather show people the thing they actually want to move. Money set aside. Distance to the goal. How close rent is to covered. That kind of progress survives a missed day, because it’s real.

It’s also the engagement worth paying for. People who can see a goal getting closer keep putting money in, and that’s the behaviour a savings product earns from.
