Operations Centre.

I built the Operations Centre for the project management office I lead, because the way we tracked our commercial pipeline was not working. Updates were spread across OneNote pages and Dynamics 365 Sales Hub, so every morning scrum started with people trying to work out what had actually happened the day before.

The Operations Centre gave business development, product and client success one place to log their work. Every update, stage movement, commitment and blocker is recorded against an opportunity as it happens, and the dashboard counts them against a daily target. Stalled deals show up on their own, commitments have an owner and a due date, and any week, month or quarter can be turned into a report and presented straight from the app instead of being rebuilt in PowerPoint.

It started with one team of about 25 people and grew to more than 45 users across three business units. The first version was a single web page that saved everything on the user’s own device, which meant we could start using it the same week. It has since grown into a full CRM with leads, accounts and goals, and the demo below shows both halves with invented data.

What it changed

  • It replaced OneNote and Dynamics 365 Sales Hub for day to day pipeline work, and brought the whole pipeline and every business development update into one place.
  • New opportunities are captured and tracked as soon as they are logged, so nobody has to copy them into a separate tracker.
  • Commitments are tracked until they are closed, and about 80% of them are.
  • Charts and reports are produced by the platform, so leadership reviews no longer depend on slides prepared by hand, and follow-up and decisions have become far more consistent.
