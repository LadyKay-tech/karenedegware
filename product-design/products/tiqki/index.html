Tiqki.

Tiqki runs the queue for small businesses: clinics, salons, banks, anywhere people stand around waiting. Customers scan a QR code at the door, join from their own phone, and watch their place move. When it is their turn, the page changes and a text arrives.

The front desk sees walk-ins and booked appointments in one list, calls the next person with one tap, and gets a feel for wait times across the day. No hardware, no app for anyone to install.

Decisions that shaped it

  • Nothing to download. Asking someone to install an app to wait ten minutes loses most of them. A QR code and a web page on their own phone loses almost nobody.
  • Walk-ins and bookings in one line. The desk works from a single list, so a booked customer never gets stuck behind the people who just walked in.
  • A text when it is your turn. People can step out for a coffee without losing their place, which makes the wait feel shorter than it is.
