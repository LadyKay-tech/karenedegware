Meeqly.

Meeqly is a place for new music to be heard. Upcoming artists upload a sixty second clip, listeners get one vote a day to spend on the songs they like, and each week the most voted songs win. At the end of the month, the overall winner gets a proper studio session.

I built it because plenty of good artists never reach anyone beyond their own followers, and the big platforms mostly reward people who already have an audience. On Meeqly everyone gets the same sixty seconds in front of listeners who came specifically to discover something, and it is those listeners, rather than an algorithm, who decide who rises.

Decisions that shaped it

  • Each listener gets one vote a day. That limit is what gives a vote its weight, it keeps spam out, and it quietly gives people a reason to come back tomorrow.
  • Every clip runs for exactly sixty seconds, which keeps the stage level for artists and makes it easy for a listener to judge a song in the gaps between other things.
  • Winners go into a Hall of Fame, so a win lasts longer than a week, and every past winner becomes a reason for the next artist to enter.
