Diva.

Diva is an AI command bar that floats above everything else on your screen. Highlight text anywhere, press Ctrl and Space or just say “Hey Diva”, and type a slash command. Diva hands the job to a specialised AI agent and streams the answer back without you leaving what you were doing.

Most AI tools make you go to them: open a tab, paste, explain, copy back. Diva comes to you. It reads what you’ve highlighted, knows the command you want, and gets out of the way when you press Escape.

Decisions that shaped it

  • Always on top, never in the way. One shortcut summons it over any app; Escape sends it away. No window switching.
  • Slash commands, not prompts. Twenty five commands, from /summarize to /actionitems, so nobody has to learn prompt writing to get a good result.
  • Your selection is the input. Whatever you’ve highlighted or copied is used automatically, so most jobs are two keystrokes.
