Duttiva.

Duttiva started with a small irritation. Every time I wanted AI to help with something I was already writing, I had to leave it to get that help: open a new tab, paste the text in, explain what I wanted, then copy the answer back into the place I started. Duttiva turns that around. It sits above everything else on your screen, and when you highlight some text and press Ctrl and Space, it opens right where you are.

From there you type a slash command such as /summarize, /reply or /actionitems, and the answer streams back in a few seconds. There are twenty five of them, which means the everyday jobs never need a prompt written from scratch. When you are done, Escape sends it away and you carry on in the document you never left. If your hands are busy, you can say “Hey Duttiva” and ask out loud instead.

Decisions that shaped it

  • It had to stay out of the way. One shortcut brings it over whatever app you are in and Escape hides it again, so it never becomes one more window to look after.
  • I chose commands over open prompts because most people do not want to learn prompt writing just to get a decent summary. Each common job became a short command that already knows what a good answer looks like.
  • Whatever you have highlighted becomes the input. That single choice is what turns most jobs into two keystrokes, where they used to take a copy, a paste and an explanation.

How to try it
  1. Pick a sample, or tap + Your own text and paste anything into the note.
  2. Highlight the part you want help with. Leave nothing highlighted and Duttiva reads the whole note.
  3. Press Ctrl + Space or tap Summon, then type / to see every command.
  4. Tap the mic and say “Hey Duttiva, summarise this”, or any other command.

This preview runs on a built-in engine that does the real work for summaries, action items, checklists, keywords, hashtags, sentiment, tables, JSON, regex and code review, on any text you give it. Writing jobs such as replying, rewriting or translating need the AI model. They are ready to try on the samples, and if you add your own OpenAI key in Duttiva’s settings (the gear), they will work on anything. The key stays in your browser. This preview is connected to a live AI model, so every command works on any text you give it. You are using your own OpenAI key, so every command runs on your account. Remove it any time in Duttiva’s settings.
