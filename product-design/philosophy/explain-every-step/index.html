Principle

Explain why you need each step.

Verification is where a lot of otherwise good products lose people, and it is rarely because the steps are difficult. More often, people are asked for their ID, a photo of their face and their bank details without a word about why any of it is needed.

A request with no reason attached feels risky, so people hesitate, abandon the process or write in to ask what is going on, and a line such as “for verification purposes” does not really answer the question.

Every step I design explains what it is for in one honest sentence, shows how much is left to do, and lets people stop and pick up where they left off. It also works as a test, because if I cannot write a convincing reason for a step, that is usually a sign it should not be there at all.

Each extra person who completes onboarding is a customer who can start paying or selling, so the explanation more than pays for itself. Losing someone at step three, on the other hand, wastes everything that was spent to bring them that far.
