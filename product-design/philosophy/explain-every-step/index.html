Principle

Explain why you need each step.

Verification is where a lot of good products lose people. The steps aren’t hard. People are asked for their ID, their face and their bank details without a word about why.

Requests with no reason feel risky, so people stall, give up, or write in to ask. “For verification purposes” isn’t an answer.

Every step I design says what it’s for in one honest sentence, shows how much is left, and lets people stop and come back. If I can’t write a good reason for a step, that’s usually a sign it shouldn’t be there.

Each extra person who finishes onboarding is a customer who can start paying, or selling. Explaining why costs nothing. Losing someone at step three costs a lot.
