Principle

Defaults are decisions you make for people.

Most people go with whatever is already selected. They’re busy, and they assume the defaults were set with them in mind. That makes every ticked box a decision I’ve made for them.

So I ask what the person would pick if they had a minute to think. Protection plans, gift wrap, paid delivery and marketing emails start unticked. Things that help everyone, like a receipt, start on.

Pre-ticking extras does lift order value, for a while. Then it comes back as refunds, chargebacks, complaints and customers who read every line next time, if there is a next time.

An honest default is a small promise that the product is on the buyer’s side. Keep it and people move through checkout faster, because they’ve learnt they don’t need to check your work.
