Principle

Show the full price before the last step.

Nothing loses trust faster than a total that grows on the last screen. Someone decides to buy at one price, hands over their address and their card, and only then finds out the real number.

I show the full price as early as I can work it out. Delivery, fees, tax, all of it. On the product page if possible, in the basket for certain. The pay button should never be the first place someone sees what they’re paying.

The worry is that this makes the product look dearer. For a second, it does. But the comparison that counts isn’t a competitor’s headline price. It’s the price the customer had in their head.

Surprise fees lose sales at the very end, after you’ve already paid to bring that person in. The ones who do pay remember how it felt. An honest total costs a little at the top and wins it back at checkout, and again on the second order.
