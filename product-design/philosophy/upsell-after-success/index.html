Principle

Upsell after success, not on a schedule.

Most upgrade prompts run on a timer. Day three, a popup. Day seven, an email. They land whenever the calendar says, which is usually while someone is in the middle of something.

The best moment to offer more is straight after the product has helped, and straight after someone has bumped into the limit the upgrade removes. Then the value is obvious without anyone selling it.

So I watch for the moment, not the date. Twenty payments sent by hand on a Friday tells you exactly who needs bulk payouts, and when to bring it up. The offer can talk about what they’ve just done, in their own numbers.

Offers timed like this convert better and annoy fewer people. Expansion revenue should feel like the product noticed, not like it nagged.
