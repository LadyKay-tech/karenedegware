Principle

Tell people exactly where their money is.

When money leaves someone’s account, they feel it straight away. Until it lands they’re holding their breath, and “processing” tells them nothing.

Vague status turns into worry, and worry turns into checking. Refreshing the app, texting the person they paid, then ringing support. Each of those is a cost, to them and to you.

I show the journey in plain steps: where the money is now, when it should arrive, and what happens if it doesn’t. A time to expect and a promise for when things go wrong do more for calm than any reassuring copy.

Clear payment status is one of the cheapest ways to cut support contacts, and one of the quickest ways to be trusted with money. People stay with the app they never had to worry about.
