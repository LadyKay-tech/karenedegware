Principle

Tell people exactly where their money is.

When money leaves someone’s account they feel it immediately, and until it arrives on the other side they are holding their breath. A status that simply says “processing” does nothing to help with that.

Vague status turns into worry, and worry quickly turns into checking: refreshing the app, messaging the person they paid and, eventually, contacting support. Each of those steps costs the customer some peace of mind and costs the business time and money.

I prefer to show the journey in plain steps, covering where the money is now, when it should arrive and what will happen if it does not. A realistic time to expect and a clear promise for when things go wrong do far more to keep people calm than any amount of reassuring copy.

Getting this right is one of the cheapest ways I know to reduce support contacts, and one of the quickest ways to be trusted with money. People tend to stay with the app they never had to worry about.
