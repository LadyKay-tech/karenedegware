Principle

A sign-up is not activation.

Somebody creating an account tells me very little. It tells me they were curious. It doesn’t tell me they found anything worth coming back for.

The moment I care about is the first time a person gets something useful out of the product. A report they can send. A number they didn’t have yesterday. That’s activation, and I design backwards from it.

Every screen standing between a new user and that moment needs a good reason to be there. If it hasn’t got one, I cut it, or I move it to after they’ve seen the value. Try both versions and you’ll feel the difference.

It’s not only a design call. People who get value early stay longer, pay sooner and bring their colleagues along. That’s where good design starts paying for itself.
