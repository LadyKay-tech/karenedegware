Principle

Make leaving easy, and listen on the way out.

How a product lets people go says a lot about how much it trusts itself. Hiding the cancel button tells customers you think they’d leave if they could find it.

A maze keeps a few people paying for one more month. It also makes sure they never come back, puts their friends off, and in more and more places it breaks the law.

I make cancelling as easy as signing up. One clear route, one honest question about why, and a pause for people who only need a break. Then I keep their things, so coming back is easy too.

Done well, the exit is the best research you’ll get. People are at their most honest when they’re leaving. One reason from everyone who goes tells you more than a year of surveys.
