Principle

Let people correct what you think you know.

Personalisation is a guess. Often a good one, but still a guess. One gift, one search for a friend, and the product decides it knows who you are.

When the guess is wrong and there’s no way to fix it, the feed fills up with things nobody wants. People stop looking, and the model learns from that silence and gets worse.

I give people quick ways to set it straight. “Not for me” on anything recommended. “Was this a gift?” after a purchase that doesn’t fit. Small, fast, and acted on straight away, so they can see it worked.

Corrections are the best signal you’ll ever get, because people tell you outright. Better picks mean more clicks, more sales, and fewer people tuning the feed out altogether.
