I’m Karen.

I work as a product designer and product manager, and I design with the business result in mind. That means thinking about how people find a product, whether they sign up, whether they pay and whether they keep using it.

I started in 2014 as a brand designer, working on logos and identities for companies. From there I moved into product design for web and mobile apps, and later into product management and strategy. I have also built products of my own and sold some of them.

Today I lead a project management office, where I oversee how work is chosen, tracked and delivered. That experience makes me a more practical designer, because I know what it takes to get a product built, launched and adopted.
