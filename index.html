I’m Karen.

I started out in 2014 designing logos and brand identities, and somewhere in those early projects I picked up a habit I have never shaken: asking whether a design is actually working for the business that paid for it.

That habit pulled me from brand work into product design, and from there into product management, where the questions got bigger and sat much closer to the money. Today I also run a project management office, which has taught me a lot about how good ideas hold up once budgets, people and deadlines get involved.

What I care about most is the point where design and commerce meet. A product should feel right in someone’s hands and still earn its keep, and in my experience you only get the second if you design for it from the very first screen.
