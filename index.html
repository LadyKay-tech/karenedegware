I’m Karen.

I started out designing logos in 2014, and I’ve never stopped asking why a design works, or doesn’t, for the business behind it.

That question took me from brand design into product design, then product management, and now into running a project management office, where I get to see how good ideas survive budgets, people and deadlines.

So I bring both halves to a product: the craft that makes it feel right, and the commercial thinking that makes it pay its way.
