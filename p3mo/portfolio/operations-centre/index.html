Operations centre.

This is a rebuild of the operations centre I designed to run a commercial pipeline day to day. The figures are made up, but the layout and the logic are the ones I use. It is built around the questions leadership asks every week: how many updates came in, which opportunities moved forward, which ones are stuck and who is holding them up.

Every opportunity sits at one of eight stages, from the first conversation to money in the bank, and belongs to an offering with a named owner. A change of stage counts as movement. Anything that has stayed in one stage longer than expected is flagged as stalled, along with the team it is waiting on, whether that is finance, legal, delivery or the client.

This is the Operations Centre itself, running with invented data. Change the date at the top, switch teams in the sidebar or add an entry to see how it responds.
