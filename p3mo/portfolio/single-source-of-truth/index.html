Portfolio · Data and reporting

Single source of truth.

Portfolio decisions are only as good as the information behind them. Before we had a single source of truth, the same pipeline lived in chat messages, personal spreadsheets, OneNote pages and a separate CRM, so every review started with a debate about whose numbers were right instead of what to do about them.

Putting every update, stage movement, commitment and blocker into one system changed how the portfolio is managed. Leadership can compare offerings and teams on the same basis, see which opportunities are moving and which have stalled, and shift people or money towards the work that is progressing. Targets, forecasts and reviews all draw on the same records, so a decision made in one meeting still holds in the next.

The dashboard here is the Operations Centre I built to be that single source of truth, running with invented data. Change the date or switch teams in the sidebar to see how the same records answer different questions.
