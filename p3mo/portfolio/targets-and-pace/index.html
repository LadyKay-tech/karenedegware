Targets and pace.

A portfolio target only helps if people can see, week by week, whether they are on course to hit it. I set targets for each period, such as revenue, new leads, qualified opportunities and contracts, and progress is tracked from the same records the teams update every day.

The weekly pace view is the part teams use most. It breaks the monthly goal down into what needs to happen this week and each day, so a team that is falling behind knows early, while there is still time to do something about it. This view comes from the CRM side of the Operations Centre, running here with invented data.
