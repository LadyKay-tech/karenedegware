Choosing what to fund.

There is always more work proposed than there is money or people to deliver it, so a portfolio needs a fair way to decide what goes ahead. I score each proposal against the same small set of criteria, agree the weights with leadership before anyone sees the scores, and then fund from the top of the list until the budget runs out.

Agreeing the weights first matters, because it moves the discussion away from whose project it is and towards what the business needs most this year. Change the weights and the budget to see which proposals make it above the line.
