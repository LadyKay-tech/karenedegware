What counts as an update.

A pipeline report is only as good as the updates that feed it, and most teams never agree on what an update actually is. When I took on pipeline reporting, the first thing I did was write that definition down and get every team working to it.

The rule is simple. An entry counts when the client can see the progress, or when the opportunity changes stage. An email chasing a client with nothing to show for it is logged as a commitment. A proposal that is ready internally only counts once it reaches the client. A brand new lead is a new entry, while an opportunity that enters straight at a later stage does count as movement.
