Monthly leadership pack.

Leadership needs the same few numbers every month, shown the same way, so they can follow the trend instead of getting used to a new format each time. My monthly pack covers updates against target, opportunity movements and where they went, how many commitments were closed and who contributed most.

The pack used to be a set of slides rebuilt by hand before every meeting. Now any week, month, quarter or year can be pulled straight from the platform and presented full screen in the room.
