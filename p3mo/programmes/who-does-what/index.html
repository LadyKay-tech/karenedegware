Who does what.

Most delays in a pipeline happen between teams rather than inside them. A proposal waits for pricing, a contract waits for legal review, a demo waits for a technical lead. Writing down who is responsible at each stage, and what each supporting team commits to, removes a lot of that waiting.

The map shows the stages of an opportunity across the top and the teams down the side. Pick a stage to see who leads it and who supports it, or pick a team to read what it is expected to contribute.
