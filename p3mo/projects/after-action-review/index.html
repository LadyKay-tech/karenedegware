After action review.

After every important client meeting, I run a short review while the details are still fresh. It records who was in the room and what they know, the problems the client described, how well we answered their questions and what happens next.

The review exists so that the follow-up actually happens and the next meeting goes better. Switch between a completed example and the blank template, and copy the template if it is useful to you.
