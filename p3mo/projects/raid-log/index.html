RAID log.

A RAID log tracks the risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies on a project. It is only useful if it stays short and current, so I review it every week with the project team and close anything that no longer applies.

Every entry has an owner and a date, and each one is scored for likelihood and impact so the most serious items sit at the top. Filter the log, sort it, close an item or add one of your own.
