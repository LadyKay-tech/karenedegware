One-page status report.

A status report should tell a busy reader three things in under a minute: whether the project is on track, what is blocking it and what decision is needed from them. Anything beyond that belongs in an appendix.

I use the same one-page format for every project so leaders always know where to look. Each area gets a red, amber or green rating with a one-line reason, the overall rating follows from those, and every blocker names the team that holds it. Change the ratings to see how the summary at the top responds.
