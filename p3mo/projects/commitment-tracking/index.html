Commitment tracking.

A lot of project slippage comes from small promises nobody wrote down: a document someone said they would send, or a call they said they would book. I treat each of these as a commitment with an owner, a due date and a link to the piece of work it belongs to.

Once commitments were tracked this way, about 80% of them were closed, and the overdue ones became visible enough to chase in the scrum. This view comes from the Operations Centre, running with invented data.
